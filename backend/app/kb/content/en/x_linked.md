title: "Changes on the X chromosome"
---
Men have one X chromosome, and women have two X chromosomes. A change on the X can pass from a mother to her sons and daughters in a different way from other genes. Sons and daughters can be affected differently, which is why X-linked results have their own explanation and picture.
