title: "What being a carrier means"
---
Most genes come in two copies, one from each parent. A carrier has one changed copy and one working copy. The working copy usually does the job, so being a carrier usually doesn't cause illness. Being a carrier matters mainly for children, if both parents carry a change in the same gene.
