title: "When both partners carry the same change"
---
If both partners carry a change in the same gene, each pregnancy has a 1 in 4 chance that the child inherits two changed copies, a 1 in 2 chance that the child is a carrier, and a 1 in 4 chance that the child inherits neither. Chance has no memory between pregnancies. Inheriting two copies doesn't affect everyone in the same way.
