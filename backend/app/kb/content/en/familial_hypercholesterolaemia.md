title: "Familial hypercholesterolaemia"
---
Familial hypercholesterolaemia means cholesterol is high from birth because of a changed gene, not because of diet. It follows a dominant pattern, so a single changed copy is enough and nobody is a carrier: somebody who has the change may be affected themselves. Each child of a parent who has it has a 1 in 2 chance of inheriting the same copy. Inheriting the copy does not affect everybody in the same way, it is usually manageable, and a doctor can explain what care is available.
