title: "Beta thalassaemia"
---
Beta thalassaemia affects how the body makes haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood. Carriers usually have no symptoms, though their red blood cells may be smaller. A child who inherits two changed copies can need regular treatment. A counsellor can explain what this means for your family.
