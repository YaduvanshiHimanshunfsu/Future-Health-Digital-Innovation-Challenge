# Lexicon Seeds

**Status:** starting list, 5 Nov 2026. **No native speaker or counsellor has reviewed it, so V1.2.10 is open.** Sub-phase 4.2 turns these into the word lists the validators read.

Arabic is written in Roman letters here under the project rule. The real lists need Arabic script, and adding that needs the team's approval.

## Words with two meanings

These caused or could cause routing errors. Anything like them needs matching by context.

| Language | Word | Meanings | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arabic | *hamil* | Pregnant, and carrier | Pregnancy only in context, such as *ana hamil*. Not before *li-* or *lil-* (C6) |
| Hindi | वाहक (*vaahak*) | Carrier, also "bearer" or "vehicle" | Safe for routing, but check it reads naturally to lay readers |
| Hindi | गर्भवती, गर्भ से | Pregnant | Route to a human. Native speaker to add everyday phrasing |

## Terms to avoid

| Meaning | Arabic (Roman letters) | Hindi | Say instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defective | *ma'ib* | दोषपूर्ण, खराब | Has a change in |
| Bad blood | *dam fasid* | खराब खून, गंदा खून | Remove |
| Incompatible | *ghayr mutawafiqayn* | असंगत, मेल नहीं खाते | Share a genetic finding |
| Deformed | *mushawwah* | विकृत | Remove |
| Disability, as a slur | *ahah* | अपंग | Use the condition's name |
| Sick, of a carrier | *marid* | बीमार, रोगी | A carrier usually doesn't have the condition |

## Directive phrasing to catch

| Language | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| English | "you should", "you must", "it would be best", "consider cancelling" | Soft forms slip past simple rules (H12) |
| Arabic, standard | *yajib alayk*, *nansahuk*, *min al-afdal* | |
| Arabic, Gulf | *lazim tsawwun*, *al-mafrud* | Dialect forms |
| Hindi | आपको करना चाहिए, ज़रूरी है, शादी मत करो, बेहतर होगा कि | Includes polite forms that still direct |

## Check for each new language

- Words with two meanings, as with *hamil*.
- Number words and local digits, for the numeric validator.
- Words for clan, caste, lineage or family honour that could stigmatise.
- Formal or everyday register, for the Simple reading level.
