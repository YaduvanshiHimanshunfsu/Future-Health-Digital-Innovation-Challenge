# Support Contacts

**Status: empty on purpose.** Each entry needs an official source and a date, confirmed by a clinician. A wrong crisis number could cost a life, so nothing here is guessed, and an LLM must never produce these (C10).

Until a pack has at least one verified contact, it can't pass V1.2.4, and the distress flow must say to contact local emergency services and alert a human.
