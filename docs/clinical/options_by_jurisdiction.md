## Prenatal diagnosis wording

Open. A clinician decides what the app says. Until then, it says what the test is and offers a counsellor, and says nothing about decisions following a result.

## Faith content policy

Only on request, per pack, written or reviewed by a qualified reviewer. With no reviewer, it's left out instead of being generated (V4.1.8).
