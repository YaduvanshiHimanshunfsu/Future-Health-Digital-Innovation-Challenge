## FAQ answers, copied word for word

**Who can apply?** Individuals, researchers, startups, developers, designers, clinicians, engineers, innovators, and multidisciplinary teams, from anywhere in the world. Team leads and award recipients must be at least 18 years old by the submission deadline. If you need permission from your employer or institution to take part, you are responsible for obtaining it.

**How big can a team be?** One to five members. You can take part on your own or as a team, and team members can be in different countries.

**Can I apply more than once?** No. Each person can be part of one team only.

**What data can we use?** The organisers will not provide any data, including synthetic data. Use only data you have the legal right to use, declare every data source, and avoid personal data you do not need.

**Who owns what we build?** Intellectual property stays with you and your team. You may be asked to share your code base where possible for evaluation purposes.

**Can we bring hardware?** Yes. The Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge suits teams that already have a working device or prototype and can develop it further. You must be able to demonstrate it safely and explain its safety and regulatory considerations.

**Do we need to be in the UAE?** No. The build runs online. The top three winning teams get to present in-person at GITEX 2026.

**Does it cost anything?** No. Registering and taking part are free.

**Can we apply with an existing product?** Yes. Ideas, existing solutions, and early prototypes are eligible for the Challenge. Tell us how mature your solution is now and what you will build or improve during the Challenge.

**Do we have to use a particular technology?** No. The Challenge is technology-neutral. Use the AI, software, sensing, or hardware that best fits the problem.

**What do we need to submit?** A working prototype by 20th November, with technical documentation and a clear statement of your assumptions, dependencies, and limitations. Full requirements are shared when you register.

**Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) of the Solution?** You retain full ownership of your IP. Whether you participate as an individual or a startup, all IP rights remain with you.
