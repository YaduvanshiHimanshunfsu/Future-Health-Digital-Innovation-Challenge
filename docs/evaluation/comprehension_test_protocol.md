# Comprehension test protocol

**Sub-phase:** 6.2. Closes S7: no simulated figures, ever. If this test doesn't run, the submission says so and shows no numbers in its place.

## Purpose

To find out, in a small first test, whether people understand a carrier result better after using Samvid. The check measures the explanation, not the person: if many people miss an item, the explanation is what changes.

## Who

- **15 to 30 adult volunteers**, aged 18 or over, reached through the team's own networks (a convenience sample, stated as such).
- English, and Arabic now that the interface has Arabic text. Aim for a spread of reading levels.
- Not invited: anyone who has a genetic condition in their close family, is pregnant, or is going through carrier screening now. The topic could be upsetting, and their real situation must not be mixed with a fictional one.

## Consent

Read out or shown before anything starts, and agreed to by ticking a box. The record keeps only the tick and the date.

> This is a test of a student prototype, not a medical service. You'll see a made-up person's genetic result, never your own. Nothing you do is linked to your name. You can stop at any time without saying why. The topic is inherited conditions and family planning; if that's difficult for you, please don't take part. Do you agree to take part?

## What is and isn't recorded

- Recorded: a random participant number, language, reading level chosen, the two teach-back scores, how long it took, and free-text comments with names removed.
- Never recorded: name, contact details, age beyond "18 or over", any health detail, or the account used. Each volunteer uses a fresh demo account deleted afterwards.

## Procedure (about 15 minutes)

1. The volunteer signs in with a fresh demo account and is given a persona (Fatima, a carrier; or Khalid and Fatima as a couple).
2. **Before:** they answer the five teach-back questions on the "Check my understanding" page without reading the result. This records the "before" score.
3. They read the result, the inheritance page, and ask Samvid one question of their own.
4. **After:** they answer the teach-back questions again. This records the "after" score.
5. Two open questions: "What was unclear?" and "Did anything feel like it was telling you what to do?"

The scores are paired per volunteer (V6.2.5): the measure is each person's own change.

## Support note

Given to every volunteer at the end:

> Thank you. Everything you saw was invented. If the topic raised worries about your own family, a genetic counsellor or your doctor is the right person to talk to. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
